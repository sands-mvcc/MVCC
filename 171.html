Framework reference

All 110 NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 requirements with their CMMC 2.0 practice identifiers. This is the corrected version of the guide you’ll audit in Mission 1.

Associate role

Mission 2: Map the evidence

FFSI’s IT lead dumped fourteen artifacts into the evidence folder with no labels. Select an artifact, then select the requirement family it supports. Each family gets exactly one artifact. Drag and drop also works.

Associate role

Mission 3: Scope the enclave

Scoping decides what the assessor will examine. Using the CMMC Level 2 scoping guidance, place each FFSI asset in its category. A wrong call either wastes assessment effort or leaves CUI unprotected.

Auditor role

Mission 4: The assessor’s bench

Six requirements, six workpapers. For each, review the evidence gathered by examine, interview, and test. Check every SP 800-171A assessment objective the evidence satisfies, then record your determination. A requirement is MET only if every objective is satisfied.

Reference: scoring and POA&M rules

Start at 110 and subtract each NOT MET requirement’s point value (5, 3, or 1). The lowest possible score is −203.

RequirementValue
3.1.8, 3.3.4, 3.6.3, 3.10.41 point each
3.5.35, or 3 if MFA is in place for remote and privileged users but not general users
3.13.115, or 3 if encryption is used but is not FIPS validated

Under 32 CFR §170.21, Conditional Level 2 status requires a score of at least 88 (80%). Only 1-point requirements may go on a POA&M, except 3.1.20, 3.1.22, 3.10.3, 3.10.4, and 3.10.5. SC.L2-3.13.11 may be POA&M’d when encryption is used but not FIPS validated.

Point values follow DoD Assessment Methodology v1.2.1. Confirm against the current version before using them on a live engagement.
