        Secure gateways and trust authorities

        Newer vehicles let any scan tool read codes and data, but lock write functions until the technician and the tool are authenticated. Practice an unlock, then compare how each manufacturer grants access.

        Programs change often. Brand coverage, fees, model years and approved tools are updated regularly. Confirm current requirements on the manufacturer’s service-information site and with NASTF before teaching or doing the work. Entries marked “Verify details” are the least settled.

        Before you start

        • An active account with the trust authority or OEM portal, in your own name. Credentials aren’t shared between technicians.
        • A scan tool registered to that account and running current software.
        • A stable internet connection: the unlock is approved online.
        • A battery maintainer if you’ll be programming.
        • Customer authorization on the repair order. Every unlock is logged to you.
