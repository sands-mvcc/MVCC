    01

    Choose 5 to 10 work roles

    The NICE Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity describes the jobs in this field as work roles. Pick the ones that sound like work you’d enjoy. Entry roles are realistic first jobs; mid and senior roles are where you can grow.

    Not sure where to start? Students finishing a certificate or associate degree are most often hired into Technical Support, Network Operations, Systems Administration, Defensive Cybersecurity, and Infrastructure Support roles.

    05

    Will you continue to a 4-year degree?

    Many senior cybersecurity roles ask for a bachelor’s degree. If you plan to transfer, choose the schools you’re considering so your advisor can check transfer agreements and course equivalencies.

    Schools you’re consideringChoose as many as you like.

    Transfer tip: A.A.S. classes are built for employment, so some may transfer only as electives. Ask the Moraine Valley Transfer Center about articulation agreements and bachelor’s completion programs designed for A.A.S. graduates.
