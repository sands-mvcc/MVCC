How to work through this lab

  1. Learn the threat. Read the overview, the real-world case and the indicators an analyst watches for.
  2. Break the attack chain. Deploy controls at different stages and launch the attack to see where it stops.
  3. Investigate the evidence. Click the log lines that show compromise or a control gap, then make the call.
  4. Triage the queue. Classify twelve mixed SOC alerts against all ten threats.
  5. Rate your readiness. Self-assess against eight analyst competency domains, then take the quiz.

By the end you can

  • Explain the ten risks and where each sits in an AI system.
  • Recognize indicators of each risk in logs, alerts and user reports.
  • Select layered controls that break each attack chain.
  • Map incidents to OWASP, MITRE ATLAS and NIST references.

    Synthesized from the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025), MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI 100-2 E2025 (Adversarial Machine Learning taxonomy), the NIST AI RMF and its Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1), and public incident reporting. Framework identifiers are current as of mid-2026; ATLAS technique IDs are periodically renamed, so confirm them at atlas.mitre.org before using them in graded work.
