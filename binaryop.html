The ten skills behind every conversion

Each conversion is a short chain of small skills. Master these in order and every conversion in this lab becomes a routine you can do on paper, on an exam, or in your head while reading a packet capture.

By the end of this lab you can

  • Explain positional notation for bases 2, 10, and 16.
  • Convert any value from 0 to 65,535 between binary, decimal, and hex without a calculator.
  • Convert IPv4 octets and subnet masks between dotted decimal and binary.
  • Read hex in MAC and IPv6 addresses as binary.
  • Check an answer by converting it back.
