Engagement brief

CUI

Does Stratum run what it wrote down?

Stratum Defense Systems wants a CMMC Level 2 certification to keep its defense contracts. You’ve been hired to do a readiness gap assessment before the official one. Your job is to compare their written policies with the systems they actually operate, and report every gap you can support with evidence.

Your role
Readiness assessor
Standard
CMMC Level 2 (NIST SP 800-171 r2)
Deliverable
Exported findings log

By the end of this case you will be able to

  • Categorize assets using CMMC Level 2 scoping categories.
  • Identify gaps between documented policy and observed configuration.
  • Map each gap to a CMMC Level 2 practice (for example, IA.L2-3.5.3).
  • Separate true findings from risks that 800-171 doesn’t require you to fix.
  • Rate findings by severity so they can feed a POA&M.

How to use this case file

  • Every evidence item has a Flag button. Flag anything you’d raise with the client.
  • Not everything is a problem. Some evidence shows controls working.
  • Flagged items collect in the Findings log, where you add the practice, severity, and your reasoning.
  • Use Notes for loose observations. Everything saves in this browser.
  • Export your findings when you’re done and submit the file.

Assessment workflow

  1. Learn the organizationSites, headcount, and what kind of CUI they handle.
  2. Scope the environmentDecide which assets store, process, transmit, or protect CUI.
  3. Review the evidenceWork through all seven evidence sections and flag what concerns you.
  4. Compare against policyRead each policy clause and check whether the configuration honors it.
  5. Write and rate your findingsMap each flag to a practice, set severity, and export.

Policies and governance

These are Stratum’s approved policies. For each clause, find the evidence that shows whether it’s being followed. A policy the company doesn’t follow is a finding in its own right.

Access Control Policy (AC-1)
Ref: POL-AC-2024-01Effective: Jan 1, 2024Owner: CISO

3.1 Purpose. To establish requirements for granting, monitoring, and revoking access to Stratum Defense Systems information assets.

3.4.2 Multi-factor authenticationMFA is mandatory for all network access originating from outside the corporate physical network. This applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors without exception. No legacy exemptions are permitted after Dec 31, 2023.
3.4.5 Remote accessSplit tunneling is strictly prohibited on all corporate VPN connections. All traffic from remote assets must tunnel through the corporate security stack for inspection.
System and Communications Protection (SC-1)
Ref: POL-SC-2024-03Effective: Mar 15, 2024Owner: IT Director
5.1.2 Unsupported softwareUnsupported or end-of-life operating systems (for example, Windows 7 or Server 2008) are prohibited on any network segment connected to the corporate WAN. Isolated “air-gapped” systems are the only exception and require written CISO approval.
5.2.1 Data loss preventionAll egress points, including email, web gateways, and USB interfaces, must have active DLP monitoring configured to detect and block the unauthorized transmission of CUI.
Audit and Accountability Policy (AU-1)
Ref: POL-AU-2024-02Effective: Feb 1, 2024Owner: Compliance Officer
4.1.3 Cloud storage loggingAccess logging must be enabled for all cloud storage containers (for example, AWS S3 or Azure Blob) that house CUI or system backups. Logs must be retained for a minimum of 365 days.
4.2.1 Third-party accessAll vendor remote access sessions must be attended (supervised) by internal IT staff. “Always-on” or unattended vendor access is strictly forbidden.

Asset scoping

Before assessing controls, decide what’s in scope. Assign each asset to a CMMC Level 2 category, then check your answers.

Categories

CUI Asset
Stores, processes, or transmits CUI. Fully assessed.
Security Protection Asset
Provides security functions for the CUI environment, even if it never touches CUI.
Contractor Risk Managed Asset
Can handle CUI but isn’t intended to, because of policy and procedure. Documented in the SSP.
Specialized Asset
OT, IoT, test equipment, government property, or restricted systems. Documented and managed through the SSP.
Out of scope
Can’t store, process, or transmit CUI and is separated from CUI assets.
