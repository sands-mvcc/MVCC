    Course basics

    Describe the course. This studio writes prompts for the course homepage and its planning documents. Each lesson, student activity, and lab page is built separately, and the homepage cards link to those files.

    Step 5 of 8

    ADA requirements

    Set the accessibility and accommodation rules the ADA accessibility specialist enforces: the laws that apply, the WCAG conformance target, the checks every page must pass, and how the course stays ready for students who receive accommodations.

    Legal framework

    Check each law and policy that applies to your institution, and confirm the list with your ADA coordinator or accessibility office.

    Conformance target

    Content that meets WCAG 2.2 AA also meets 2.1 AA. Version 2.2 adds criteria such as focus not obscured, minimum target size, and accessible authentication.

    Accessibility checks to enforce

    WCAG success criteria are listed in parentheses. Criteria tagged 2.2 are applied as best practice when the target is 2.1.

    Accommodation support

    The disability services office approves accommodations, not the instructor. These settings make the course ready to apply them without redesign.

    Disability services contact

    Used in the homepage accessibility statement. Leave blank and the team inserts a clearly marked placeholder.

    How the team verifies accessibility

    This studio writes prompts and does not give legal advice. Ask your ADA coordinator or accessibility office to confirm which requirements and compliance dates apply, and to review finished pages.
